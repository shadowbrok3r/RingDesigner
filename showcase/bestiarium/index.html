Arachne

The weaver

A spider clasps the finger at the hub of her own orb web, on a keyframed low dome 5.6 × 2.4 mm. A 9 × 7 mm onyx abdomen and a 5 × 3.5 mm garnet carapace sit in collets, eight jointed legs grip the band, and the silk runs round the shoulders to the palm.

Manticora

The tail that throws

The ring is the manticore's scorpion tail, on a keyframed high dome 5.4 × 2.2 mm. Graded tergites rise from the palm to a venom bulb where a 7 × 5 mm ruby sits in a beaded collet, the hooked sting curls over the stone, and graded black spinel princesses run down each shoulder.

Lost wax · ruby and ten black spinels
