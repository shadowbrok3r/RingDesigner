Manufacturing notes

Cast as one solid cushion signet. Part on the broad Z=0 plane; lift along +Z. Preserve the bore's sand island. The preliminary gate feeds the heavier head; verify feeding with the caster. Manufacturing notes

Investment cast the pierced head and shank separately. Keep the underside open for investment removal; inspect the 1.3 mm seal roof and gallery rails. Feed each part using a caster-designed tree. Fit the head feet to the shank, solder or laser join, and inspect both joints. Ease shank edges by hand, polish the octagonal rim, and satin the seal. The nominal assembly overlaps at fitting stock; use the separate component pattern files for manufacture.

Manufacturing notes

Investment cast the continuous half-turn ribbon. The changing section traps a rigid sand mold in a straight pull. Feed at the thick palm segment; preserve the twist when removing the sprue. Smooth and polish the uninterrupted ribbon, ease the bore edge, and verify the minimum measured bore after finishing. No separate overlapping bands or hidden assembly joints.

What the checks establish. Aster and Tide have zero sampled obstructions in both mold halves at 0.100 and 0.075 mm ray pitch, no unresolved rays, and valid closed meshes. Their status remains “Review” because low-draft surfaces need a physical mold trial. The investment designs are blocked in all six tested axis-aligned sand orientations; this does not rule out specialized cores or other molding methods.

Before manufacture. Calibrate shrink for the actual process and alloy. Wall values are radial samples for the sand rings and sampled local normals for the CAD parts; unsampled details still need inspection. Lantern’s weight sums its separate parts before fitting; its source assembly contains intentional contact stock. Use its two component patterns and review the joints at the bench. The collection is modeled and inspected, not physically cast.
